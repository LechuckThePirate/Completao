# Completao!!

**Every quest, in order.** Completao!! lays out the quests of Classic WoW as
chain trees: what each quest needs before you can take it, where it starts, what
it asks of you, and which ones you have already done. Pick a section on the
left, read the tree on the right, click a quest for everything about it -- and
let the addon walk you to the person who hands it out.

Dungeons, raids, battlegrounds, zones, class and profession quests, races and
world events are in, with more on the way.

![Completao!! 2.0: the chain trees of a zone](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/main_window.png?v=2)

## New in 2.0: a whole new look

Version 2 redraws every window from scratch: obsidian and burnished gold, a
side panel on burnt parchment, quests framed as stone plates with a glow for
their state, and a floating tracker that stays out of your way. Everything you
had is still here -- your progress, your filters, your settings -- and a lot more
came with it:

- Chain trees that read **top to bottom**, with each chain in a band of its own
  and a **chain view** that shows one chain alone.
- A **list view** for the big zones, and **one set of filters** shared by every
  view -- or one per view, as you prefer.
- A quest page that opens in a **drawer** beside the tree, with the quests it
  needs one click away.
- A lighter **focus window** with a direction arrow and a bar behind every
  objective.
- Distances in **meters or yards by your region**, like TomTom's, and a
  preferences window in four tabs.

## Features

### Quest chain trees
Each entry -- a dungeon, a raid, a zone -- draws its quests top to bottom, by
prerequisite, so a long chain reads like a story instead of a list. **Every
chain has a band of its own**, so the lines that join its quests never run over
someone else's. Each quest is a stone plate whose frame and glow say its state:

- **iron with a green glow**: available now, or in your log (the one you are doing pulses);
- **bronze with a golden glow**: completed;
- **rusty iron in chains**: locked, with what it still needs written on it;
- **polished gold**: the quest whose details are open.

Under its title a plate says its level and zone (or its state), and along the
bottom, the money, the items and the XP it gives. Connections are iron **rails**:
with glowing runes along the path you have walked and the chain you selected,
plain where you have yet to go, with a gem at every bend and an arrowhead into
each quest. Only the quests of your own faction are shown.

Drag the background -- or any quest -- to pan; the mouse wheel zooms, centered
on the cursor (Shift + wheel to go sideways, Ctrl + wheel vertically), and the
buttons in the tree's corner zoom, reset the zoom and **center on your current
quest**. A tree that fits the view is drawn in its middle. Your zoom is
remembered. A button next to them puts the tree on the **whole screen**,
without the side panel.

![A chain, on its own, with the quest's details beside it](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/chain_view.png?v=2)

### One chain at a time
In a crowded tree it is hard to follow a chain by eye. Click a quest that is
part of one and the tree redraws with **that chain alone**, whole, with the
chain's name in the title and a **Back to all quests** button next to the
filters. The same happens from a search result, from **View chain** and from
other addons.

![The tree on the whole screen](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/tree_fullscreen.png?v=2)

### A side panel that grows with the addon
At the top, the views -- **Search quests**, **Quest Log**, **Tracked
Quests**, **Saved Quests** and **Quests to Turn In** -- the last four with how
many quests they hold. Under them, the sections: **Dungeons**, **Raids** (Molten Core, Blackwing
Lair, Naxxramas, Ahn'Qiraj, Zul'Gurub...), **Battlegrounds**, **Zones**, **Class
Quests**, **Professions**, **Races**, **Events** (Lunar Festival, Darkmoon Faire,
the Ahn'Qiraj war effort, Scourge Invasion...) and **Miscellaneous**. A box above
them filters the entries by name, so you don't scroll through 34 zones, and
every entry shows its level range, a thin bar of **how much of it is done**
(`done/total`) and a **NEW** tag on what Forever adds on top of Classic. Apart
from dungeons and raids, sections only list what you can actually do. The panel
collapses to a strip of icons with the arrow in its corner. A bar in the
window's header adds up **everything you have done** across all the data.

### A list when a tree is too much
Zones like Stranglethorn or the Barrens have far too many quests for one
picture. Switch the toolbar from **Tree** to **List** and the entry's quests
come as a table -- the same one as the search -- with status icons, level, zone,
distance, XP and rewards, sortable by any column. **Chains only** keeps just the
quests that belong to a chain. Your choice is remembered.

![A zone as a list](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/list_view.png?v=2)

### Only what applies to you
Quests of another class, race or faction stay out of your way. If you want to
see them, look at that class or race in particular -- each has its own entry --
or tick **Show other faction** to browse the opposite faction's zones.

### Filters that say what they do
Every view that lists quests has the **same filters**, with the same words:
**Hide low level** (grey), **Hide too high** (quests you can't take yet),
**Hide completed**, **Hide repeatable done**, **Show other faction**,
**Chains only** and **Only quests with item rewards**, plus a **reward type** --
say, *Weapon > Wands*. A line under the search box tells you, in words, which
filters are in force (click it to change them), so you always know why a quest is
missing. Each view keeps its own; tick **Same filters in every view** in the
preferences to use one set everywhere.

By level, chains are shown or hidden as a whole, so they are never cut in half:
a chain is hidden as "too high" when everything it starts with is above your
level, and as "low level" only when every quest in it is grey. A chain you have
already started, and anything in your quest log, is never hidden by level. Your
choices are remembered, per character or for all of them.

### Search every quest at once
**Search quests...**, at the top of the side panel, looks through every
dungeon, raid, zone and class at the same time. Results come as a table with
level, location, distance, XP, money and the reward icons (hover for the item
tooltip), sortable by any column; click one to see its details beside the table,
or its **chain icon** to jump to its tree. Every row starts with the quest's
`[level]` (`D` for dungeon quests, `+` for elite ones), and the **Distance**
column shows how far each quest's next step is from you -- sort by it and the
table re-sorts as you move. When the quest page opens beside the table, the
least needed columns step aside (rewards first, then money, XP...) so the titles
stay readable. Each table has its own title box: a search in the quest log only
looks at the quest log.

![Searching quests](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/search.png?v=2)

### Your quest log, and the quests you track
**Quest Log** shows the quests you carry in the same table, with a status icon
(ready to hand in / in progress) next to each, colored by difficulty for your
level, and can **group** them by zone, level, status or section, with
**Expand all** and **Collapse all** for the groups.

![The Quest Log, grouped by zone](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/quest_log.png?v=2)

**Tracked Quests** narrows it to the ones in your objective tracker and lists,
under each quest, its objectives with live progress: finished ones checked, the
next one in gold, and only the turn-in once the quest is ready. Click an
objective to set your waypoint on it. **Quests to Turn In** lists what is ready
to hand in, nearest first.

![The Tracked Quests view](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/tracked_quests.png?v=2)

**Saved Quests**, below Tracked Quests, is yours to fill: **Save** a quest from
its details (or **Save** / **Unsave** from the right-click menu) to keep it
there, whether you have taken it or not, so you can look it up again at any
time without having to search for it.

### The focus window
**Right-click** a tracked quest (or use **Focus** in its details) to put it in a
small floating window of its own, with no heavy frame: its status icon and
title, an **arrow that points to the step your waypoint is on, with the
distance**, and the objectives below, each with a thin bar behind it that fills
as you go ("Boars 3/7"), struck through once done. The waypoint goes on the
nearest objective left and moves by itself as you finish them; with everything
done the list turns into `Turn in <quest> (<npc>)`. The title bar -- the padlock,
the spyglass that puts the focus back on the nearest tracked quest, and the
close button -- only shows while the mouse is over the window. Under the quest,
**Also here** lists the other tracked quests with something to do by the same
spot, and folds away with a click. Drag it anywhere (also by its objectives), and
in combat it lets every click through. With **Live focus** (Preferences) the
focus follows you, switching to the nearest tracked quest as you play.

![The focus window](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/focus_window.png?v=2)

### Repeatable quests stand out
Repeatable quests have a **blue** title in the trees, carry an **R** in the lists
(`[30R]`) and say *Repeatable* in their details. They don't count in the
progress (the game never marks them done), and **Hide repeatable done** puts
aside the ones you have already turned in once -- the ones you have never done
still show.

### Don't lose a quest by taking another
Some quests disappear for good once you take another one: a lead-in like
*Westbrook Garrison Needs Help!* is gone after you take *Riverpaw Gnoll Bounty*
from Deputy Rainer, and some quests exclude each other. Completao!! warns you
under **Careful** in their details and tooltip -- also on the quest that would
make the lead-in disappear -- before it happens. Once lost, they show in red as
*No longer available* and leave the progress.

### When a quest needs one from somewhere else
Plenty of quests are not given until you have done one in another zone or
dungeon -- *The New Plague*, in the Ruins of Lordaeron, wants the whole *A
Recipe For Death* chain from Silverpine Forest first. There is no line in the tree
to tell you, so the quest's plate says it in red -- **Requires: A Recipe For
Death (3/3)** -- and its details list it as a link, with a chain icon, that
opens the quest you need in its own tree.

### Consolidate alts
Turn on **Consolidate alts**, the pill in the window's header, and the
whole addon looks at all your characters as one -- so you can finish every zone
across your account. What any of them has done counts as completed in the
trees, the progress and the search (with *Completed by* and the names of the
characters who did it, in their class colors, in its tooltip and details), also
for the quests it unlocks; and the quests, classes, races, professions and
factions of all of them are shown. The quests too low or too high are still the
ones of the character you play. Each character is remembered once it logs in.
Only the characters of the same ruleset go together: the same realm, and
Hardcore ones only with Hardcore ones.

### Which ones are done inside, and which are ready to hand in
Quests that take place inside a dungeon or raid wear a small **doorway icon** on
the top-left corner of their plate, with a note in the tooltip and the quest
panel -- so you can tell them from the ones you do outside on the way there. A
quest in your log with every objective done gets a green **check** on the
top-right corner, so you know at a glance what is only waiting for the turn-in.

### Everything about a quest, one click away
Click any quest to open its page in a **drawer** on the right, so the tree or the
table stays in sight beside it:

- **Requirements** -- prerequisite quests and level, in green when you meet
  them and red when you don't, each quest a link to its own tree.
- **Objective** -- what to do, and **Steps**: start, each objective with the
  zone where it is done, and turn-in.
- **Who starts it and who ends it**, with the zone and a **map pin** that marks
  the spot.
- **Description** -- the story behind it; for the quests in your log,
  straight from the game in your language.
- **Rewards** -- the items you can choose and the ones you always get, with the
  game's own item tooltip (Shift-click to link one in chat), plus money,
  experience and reputation.
- Six plates at the foot, one word each (the whole action in the tooltip):
  **Focus**, **Waypoint**, **Chain**, **Save**, **Map** and **Abandon**.
- A **maximize / restore** button to read the page over the whole window.

![A quest's details](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/quest_details.png?v=2)

### Find it, go there
- **Waypoint** is a split button that already points at the next step: the
  quest giver (or the quest you still need first), the first objective you
  haven't finished, or the turn-in when you're done. The arrow lists every step
  to pick another. Works with [TomTom](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/tomtom)
  or Waypoint Tracker, or uses the game's own waypoint if you have neither.
- **Map**, and the pins next to a quest's start and end, open the world map on
  that step and drop a bouncing gold **!** marker on the exact spot, easy to see
  even on a busy map. A step inside an instance? The button says **Entrance**
  and takes you to the door of the dungeon instead.
- Don't have the quest yet, or just want to find it again later? **Save** keeps
  it in **Saved Quests**, a view of its own below Tracked Quests in the side
  panel. Already have it? Right-click a quest for **Open quest**, which opens
  your quest log right on it.
- Distances read in **meters or yards by your region** -- yards in the US, meters
  elsewhere, like the game's own waypoint and TomTom -- or the unit you pick in
  the preferences.

![The gold marker on the world map](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/map_marker.png?v=2)

![A TomTom waypoint to the quest giver](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/tomtom_integration.png?v=2)

### Made to stay out of your way
- **Quest helpers** can be switched off -- in Preferences, with a shift-click
  on the minimap button or with `/completao helpers` -- for those who quest
  without help and only want the lists of what is left: no focus window,
  waypoints or map markers pop up while questing. The main window stays as it is.
- Move and resize the window, or **minimize** it to its header; its position,
  size, open section and selected dungeon are remembered **per character** (or
  shared, if you prefer).
- **Fades while you move**, like the world map: the window goes half transparent
  while you walk or run, so it never hides what is ahead, and comes back when you
  stop or when the cursor is over it. `/completao fade <10-100>` changes how
  much (100 = no fade).
- **Click-through**: let the mouse pass through the window in combat and/or
  while you move (separate settings), with its own opacity in combat.
- **Sync with Blizzard Quest Log**: the window opens and closes with the game's
  quest log, and selecting a quest there opens it -- and its chain -- here.
- Completion is always read live from the game, so it is never out of date.
- A minimap button opens it (drag it wherever you like).

![The minimap button](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/minimap_button.png?v=2)

### Preferences, in four tabs
The gear in the window's header opens them: **General** (the minimap button,
the startup chat messages, hiding empty or completed categories, syncing and
opening on the quest log, and the **distance units**), **Interface & Opacity**
(the opacity at rest, while moving and in combat, and click-through),
**Tracker & Waypoints** (the quest helpers, autofocus and live focus, Blizzard's
tracker, Tab targeting) and **Profile / Data** (keep them for one character or
share them, and one set of filters for every view). The buttons at the foot
reset the window position, the zoom and the filters.

![The preferences](https://media.joanvilarino.online/completao/images/screencaps/preferences_interface.png?v=2)

Commands: `/completao` (or `/cpl`) opens the window, `/completao prefs` the
preferences, `/completao minimap` shows or hides the minimap button,
`/completao fade <10-100>` sets the opacity while you move, `/completao helpers`
turns the quest helpers on or off. You can also bind
keys to open the window and the preferences, in their own **Completao!!** section
of **Options -> Keybindings**.

## Where the data comes from

Quest data is generated, not typed in by hand: the Classic quests come from
Questie's database; the ones Forever adds, and the rewards of every quest, are
compiled from public quest databases. That has a consequence you should know about:

- Every Classic dungeon, raid, battlegrounds, zone, class and profession has
  its quests, as do the world events, and so do
  the ~700 quests Forever adds: in their zones and classes, and in the new
  **Zephras Isle**, **Camping** and **Crafting** entries.
- Of Forever's new dungeons, **The Hall of Thanes** and **Ruins of Lordaeron**
  have theirs so far; the other new dungeons and the new raids appear as their
  quests are published, and coverage grows with each update.
- Quest text shipped with the addon is English only; quest titles follow your
  client's language, and so does the description of the quests in your log.
- Some quests start inside an instance and have no known starting point yet --
  for those the addon shows the entrance instead.
- Some requirements between quests of different zones may be missing from the
  data: when a quest giver won't give you a quest the addon says is available,
  please tell us.

Expect gaps, and please report them.
